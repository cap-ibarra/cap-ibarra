# Voice

The cap-ibarra voice is calm, welcoming, and thoughtful.

Never loud.

Never overly technical.

Never arrogant.

The goal is to make visitors feel like they are stepping into a cozy workspace rather than reading a résumé.

---

Examples

Greeting

Good evening.

Closing

Until next commit.

Tone

Warm.

Curious.

Minimal.

Intentional.
